# Kowloon demo concept art

- [Industrial daylight paintover](industrial-daylight.png) is the current
  direction: bright hazy daylight, blue-green shadows, restrained plaster loss,
  folded ventilation ducts, brackets and localized service grime.
  [Generation prompt](industrial-daylight.md)

- [Blue-hour street paintover](blue-hour-street.png) covers the original courtyard
  frontages and shop row. [Generation prompt](blue-hour-street.md)

  corner house, the side tenement, and the upper tenement. The two companion
  studies follow the four-sided trapezoid direction.

The concept art lives here for future facade and prop iteration. Compiled
scenes use the procedural geometry and material library.

[Detail crops and review targets](details/README.md) retain the precise source
areas for close comparison of new assemblies, starting with the industrial ducts.

## Lane tenement sheet

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool from the real-time lane and corner
captures and the first street paintover.

The exact original prompt was not retained. The study brief was to preserve
the reference corner house, use four-sided trapezoid companion buildings, and
match their cages, service details and lived-in finishes to the corner house.
This is a summary, not a verbatim generation prompt.
