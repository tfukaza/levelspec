Every new assembly should have an identified concept crop and a matching close
render reviewed before scene-wide acceptance. Check silhouette and proportions,
connections and support, material scale and local wear separately. Overall
building screenshots supplement this comparison.

The procedural broad duct uses the main assembly as its target. Its periodic
color/normal/metallic-roughness maps are generated by
`tools/bake-vent-metal.ts`, with one shared set of three 1024 px maps over two
meters for all buildings (512 texels/m). Brackets, flanges and fasteners use the
same matte zinc finish family. Broad thin oxidation preserves visible substrate;
actual surface-distance UVs retain its physical size on different modules and
sloped bends. Rounded geometry and one shared scalar polished-edge finish supply
edge catches. Parameterized procedural wall deposits add broad washes and
unequal branching runoff around connections and bracket anchors. See the
[material and runoff study](../duct-material-study/README.md).

The ribbon-window kit uses 10 cm ceramic spandrels with fine grout sampled at
256 texels/m on these small receiving panels. Tiles add no per-tile meshes or
images. Glazing variation has an independent seeded stream, preserving sash
placement. Stored planters have tapered bodies, raised rims and recessed soil;
their clusters leave clear portions of the fitted cage. The daylight review
reduces broad blue fill while retaining the sunlit key, with tighter screen-space
occlusion and restrained painted contact halos. Brown runoff remains separate
from contact shading.
