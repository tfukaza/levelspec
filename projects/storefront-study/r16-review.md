# R16 independent visual review

The lighting-source correction is meaningful, but R16 is **not yet a visual match** to the concept. This review does not substitute geometry tests or source inventories for visual quality.

## Evidence inspected

- Target: `concepts/paintover-r13/roast-cutaway.png` (AI concept art, not a model capture).
- Frozen actual model: `../../generated/storefront-study/r16/roast-cutaway.png`, `roast-interior.png`, and `roast.png`.
- Fixed-exposure source isolation: `sources-daylight.png`, `sources-fixtures.png`, `sources-combined.png`, and `source-comparison.png` in that revision directory.
- Paired complete images created with Sharp: `../../generated/storefront-study/r16/roast-paintover-comparison.png`. Neither panel was retouched. Camera/framing differences remain, so this is a qualitative art comparison rather than a pixel-registration test.

## Lighting judgment

The fixture-only panel visibly supplies the central-counter light pool; daylight-only leaves that worktop subdued. The full interior shows actual fluorescent battens above this area. The prior impression of an unexplained central light now has a credible modeled source. The cutaway hides those ceiling fittings for inspection, so it is insufficient by itself to judge source motivation. Cast shadows from fixtures, stock and furniture visibly contribute grounding.

This does not establish full physical lighting accuracy. The fixture-only image has near-black upper surfaces and sharp point-source shadows, reflecting the absence of indirect bounce and the use of center samples for extended tubes. The combined image uses low ambient approximation. Those are real-time limitations, not evidence of a physically solved enclosed room.

## Required improvements, in priority order

1. **Move the lighting emphasis from the rear wall and bare center counter to the food display.** In the full storefront, the strongest interior patch is the nearly white tile/order-slip area below the rear hood. Most hanging ducks remain dark brown, with small bright patches at their necks. The concept has warm, legible golden bodies and a more restrained rear work zone. Reduce hood spill onto vertical tile, and aim or reposition the modeled display lamps so their pools cover bird bodies rather than only their tops. Lower central batten intensity relative to the display if necessary. Do not repair this with another invisible room fill. Verify the revised result in source isolation and the street view, retaining readable food texture without clipped white worktops.

2. **Break up the long, bright, sparsely occupied center work surface.** R16's central timber peninsula is an unusually continuous light stripe with a takeaway stack, one bowl stack and a small upper fixture. The concept uses layered bowls, tins, trays and steamers, producing occupied volume and interrupted highlights while preserving a usable plating zone. Add purposeful grouped stock to the upper shelving and selected ends of the counter, keeping a clear working patch and both passages. This is a density/composition gap, not permission to fill circulation.

3. **Give the rear cooking assembly a stronger hood shape and material response.** The actual hood reads as a thin dark horizontal cap with a simple chimney; the concept has a substantial sloped, ribbed, grease-worn enclosure. A thicker folded/sloped hood with readable filters and localized worn metal would give the kitchen a recognizable focal silhouette. Its under-hood light should illuminate woks and work surface without making the entire backsplash the brightest object.

4. **Replace uniform finish repetition with localized use wear.** The actual small wall tiles and floor now establish useful scale, and the earlier black triangular wall corruption is absent in the inspected R16 images. However, broad ceramic fields remain evenly gridded, the green dado has repeated small isolated chip spots, and the floor is uniformly matte. The concept has larger irregular edge wear, seam deposits, streaking beneath services and limited reflected warm light. Localized roughness/grime variation would help more than globally darkening every surface or adding uniformly distributed damage.

## Scope of conclusion

R16 materially improves source attribution and removes conspicuous wall corruption. It does not yet preserve the concept's warm food-focused hierarchy, dense central storage, substantial kitchen equipment or irregular worn surfaces. Lighting should receive the next focused correction first; the other gaps remain substantive art work, not optional polish. No circulation or performance acceptance is claimed by this visual review.
