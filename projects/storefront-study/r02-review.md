# Storefront fidelity review — r02

Independent review of the frozen r02 production renders against the three supplied concepts. **Improved, but not yet close enough to accept as a reference match.**

## Evidence

Ran `node projects/storefront-study/compare.mjs r02` and inspected all three combined images under `generated/storefront-study/r02/`. The script preserves complete reference/render images, their aspect ratios, and labels both panels; it does not retouch the render. Input paths and operation are recorded in `comparisons.json`, with frozen geometry hashes in the build/capture manifests. The r01 review was consulted only to distinguish progress from remaining defects.

## What visibly improved

- The awning now reads as a shallow canvas surface rather than deeply corrugated material. Dusty red/cream striping is substantially closer.
- Provisions shelves now show many small retail facings rather than a wall of shipping boxes. The front sack barrier is gone and the entrance reads more clearly.
- Roast birds have gained mass, and the cabinet spans more of the counter with a credible glazed roof.
- The closer camera gives the shops more image area. Fine-detail comparisons are more useful than in r01.

## Shared next repairs, in priority order

1. **Distribute wear across the scene, at more than one scale.** The procedural lintel has large peeling islands, while the cream pier fields, interior walls, low charcoal plinth, pavement and threshold remain smooth and clean. The references have brown moisture/grease streaks, dirty joints, mottled remaining paint, chipped corners and rubbing around contact zones. Add this middle/small-scale wear before adding more giant flakes. Pavement needs visible joints and a worn curb rather than one continuous dark rectangular slab.
2. **Give the room a shaded depth hierarchy.** The pale left interior wall is conspicuously bright in both grocery views. The references concentrate readable highlights on merchandise and fixtures within a darker envelope; the current even fill makes the room resemble a display stage. Reduce broad interior bounce and retain modest, localized fluorescent/task lighting. In the duck case specifically, warm highlights must distinguish glossy food and glass from the shaded tiled workroom.
3. **Finish the awning and architectural frame.** The new flat silhouette is much better, but stripes are still too clean and uniform, the hem has little abrasion, and the facade reads as a thin isolated panel. Add irregular fading/dirty runoff at seams and a few worn hem sections, narrow aged-green opening framing, and the heavier overhead/reveal depth visible in the concepts. Camera framing still exposes beige margins and the entire rectangular base; tighten it only after extending/cropping the architectural context. Do not compensate for the remaining camera/context difference by enlarging every prop.

## Provisions / liquor: three highest-impact next repairs

1. **Replace the right foreground counter dominance with the reference's tall open retail edge.** The concept's full-height narrow wire rack is a major silhouette and density anchor. R02 still presents a large polished brown counter across the right foreground; its new narrow rack is visually lost at the edge. Move/recess the counter and make the stocked rack tall, open-sided and clearly visible, with tins/jars/bottles from floor level to the head while preserving the entry.
2. **Build the left foreground mass with varied crate stacks and cartons.** The refrigerator and small green stack dominate a low, sparse left edge. The concept has two or three worn red/green crate columns reaching roughly waist/chest height, a small stack of stained cartons, and a leaning handtruck. Add supported stacks with a varied skyline and coherent per-crate colors. Do not return to a continuous barrier across the entrance.
3. **Layer the depth and suppress the tidy retail-grid effect.** The back wall now has the right merchandise category, but its even long shelf grid and broad clean opening still read simpler than the concept. Strengthen the visible side rack banks, vary shelf-front stock group height/setback, add a restrained hanging cloth/dried-goods cluster at left, and make the continuous bright hanging packet fringe less dominant. Small local irregularity should retain shelf support; random scattering is not a substitute.

## Produce: three highest-impact next repairs

1. **Raise and broaden the produce display mass.** The unchanged shallow tray grid on a continuous timber staircase remains the largest mismatch. The concept uses taller front support crates, fewer broad overfilled heaps, irregular leafy outlines and higher rear piles. Preserve per-crate finish variation, but give produce enough volume to obscure parts of the rims. Coordinate with the produce agent; do not stretch fruit or adopt a rejected mesh solely for density.
2. **Fill the blank rear/side wall band with pantry storage.** The area above the current carton row remains almost empty. Add tight stocked back/side shelves, upper cartons, and a few supported/suspended dried goods. The narrow right aisle should remain legible and shaded, with storage along its sides rather than across its path. This will improve crowdedness more than adding more low trays.
3. **Rebalance overhead features.** Tiny spaced banana hands and four vivid red pendants contrast with the reference's larger hanging bunches, restrained fluorescent strips and prominent suspended scale. Increase the banana group mass within realistic clearances, reduce the red lamps' dominance, and bring the scale/pan into the display–aisle junction. Keep fresh produce color separate from dirt on supporting crates, floor and fittings.

## Roast duck: three highest-impact next repairs

1. **Match the asymmetrical case/door composition.** R02 still has the service opening on the right, whereas the reference has a worn green framed door at left and the broad case at right. Select the mirrored arrangement for this comparison. The roof is an improvement, but the surrounding case/head junction should read integrated with the opening rather than a loose thin frame inside it. Keep the rear accessible for service.
2. **Make the birds unmistakably the focal food display.** Scale improved, but the short round birds still read as chickens rather than the reference's longer hanging ducks. They need elongated neck/body/leg silhouettes, deeper lacquered amber color and localized glossy highlights. Use fewer appropriately spaced larger silhouettes if needed. Confirm hook attachment, separation, and clearance from glass and trays from other views; the front image alone does not establish those facts.
3. **Replace the clean aqua stripe emphasis with grimy ivory and counter layering.** The cyan tile bands and large black cooking forms are visually stronger than the birds and serving sill. The concept has aged off-white tiles with brown deposits at grout/base, warm metal/glass reflections, stacked bowls/plates, a readable chopping block and meat trays. Shift tile colors toward dirty ivory, move the large oven's visual weight deeper, and make crockery/food groupings on the sill more substantial without filling the work area indiscriminately.

## Acceptance boundary

R02 establishes worthwhile structural improvements, especially for the awning and provisions stocking, but density and material age still differ substantially from the references. The next comparison should prioritize room/pavement grime plus one principal composition fix per trade. This review assesses only the supplied street view. It does not certify hidden support, intersections, circulation, collision, navigation, other elevations or runtime cost.
