# R05 critical reference review

Reviewed the frozen R05 PNGs after pairing them with their supplied concepts using `node projects/storefront-study/compare.mjs r05`. The paired images preserve each complete source image, aspect ratio, and identical panel dimensions. This is visual art assessment, not navigation certification.

## What improved

- The provisions shop now visibly continues into depth. The intermediate shelf bank prevents the former empty-hall impression and creates successive layers of stock.
- The produce shop has a legible right-side opening, taller front crate supports, and a stocked background. It reads as a shop rather than an isolated stepped display.
- The roast shop has deeper preparation/storage layers, a left service opening, ivory stained tiles, and more slender amber birds. These are closer to the target composition than R01–R04.

The deeper rooms are a useful improvement. Their front arrangements still determine whether that depth is visible; the grocer communicates it most clearly. Produce depth is visible through a narrow slot and the duck shop's counter hides much of its working floor, consistent with their shop types.

## Three highest-impact attainable art repairs

1. **Match the visible shop scale before judging small details.** The concept images crop close to the bay, whereas the procedural captures include substantial neutral backdrop, a tall top strip, and the whole paving platform. Move the camera closer and aim slightly lower, retaining the same modest right-side angle. Keep an uncropped inspection capture separately. This is a presentation discrepancy, not evidence that the shop geometry is too small.
2. **Build larger, trade-specific threshold masses.** The provisions reference has a waist/chest-high left crate group and a full-height open stock rack on the right. R05 has low sparse crate groups, prominent tiny fruit stands, and broad dark timber shelf ends. `display-assemblies.ts` currently chooses a produce tray for grocer boards with 65% probability; the liquor/provisions comparison should emphasize bottle crates, tins, and cartons. Retain a continuous entrance, but occupy more of its flanking edges. The produce frontage is substantially closer now and does not need another wholesale rearrangement.
3. **Weather the visible contact regions at smaller scales.** Broad cream piers, the near left interior wall, and the awning hem are still relatively smooth and even. The current large peeling patches on the top beam do not substitute for the reference's handled edges, brown damp deposits near pavement, chipped charcoal plinth, dirty frame joints, and dusty/abraded canvas bands. Prioritize these foreground surfaces over adding more tiny stock. Preserve distinct material response: dull canvas, chalky plaster, rubbed metal and greasy food should not share one flat brightness.

## Trade notes

### Provisions

Density and depth are markedly better. The center shelf's large unbroken timber end remains a visual divider unlike the reference's open shelf ends and overlapping labels. A narrow open rack at the right threshold and taller left crates would better match the source without closing its entrance. The concept's saturated colors are mostly small package accents surrounded by aged neutral surfaces; R05 has more clean high-contrast packet rows and brighter green/blue crate accents.

### Produce

The open aisle is a meaningful correction. The foreground now has the right basic scale of stacked boxes. Organic stock still reads as repeated small geometric groups: the source has broader leafy heaps, irregular root silhouettes, and much larger hanging banana hands. Those remain primarily asset-quality differences for the produce agent rather than reasons to alter the new room depth.

### Roast duck

The new bird proportions and amber finish help, but the body/head silhouette still reads as a repeated stylized chicken asset rather than the reference's long-neck whole ducks. Glossy highlights remain restrained compared with the reference. A weathered green half-glazed service door at the left opening would be a relatively inexpensive identity cue. Counter crockery could be grouped more prominently at one end; the reference has a clear white plate stack and larger chopping block. Do not duplicate the already queued R06 cabinet-lid, suspension, and prep-clearance fixes in this art pass.

## Assessment

Changes required for a close reference match. R05 succeeds at improving depth, density and shop identity, but should be described as a stronger procedural interpretation rather than a finished close reconstruction. Remaining limits are material microdetail, organic mesh fidelity and close camera correspondence.
