## Latest restaurant correction

The peninsula materially improves the kitchen. R10's broad, weakly defined central void is now a recognizable galley: preparation against the wall, plating and closed storage on the other side, and cooking at the back. The kitchen occupies less width, while diners have a visibly separate route beside the tables. The low counter preserves a view toward the cooking line. This resolves the visual organization issue without filling walking space with arbitrary boxes.

Two woks are clearly identifiable on the rear cooking surface, and one open sink stands beside the roaster. The till is grounded at the entrance end of the display counter. The default view contains two tables and four stools; the 4.4 by 5.2 m boundary capture reduces this to one table with two stools. Those changes are legible in the actual model, not merely asserted by the concept.

One R11 defect remains at the rear turn: the loose gas cylinder stands in front of the wok line near the peninsula end. It visually occupies part of the cook's standing/turning space. The architecture reviewer independently identified that obstruction. The parent reports its removal for R12; this review does not treat an uninspected later revision as accepted. The kitchen remains long, but its working organization is now plausible; further wholesale rearrangement is not needed to address the user's latest complaint.

## Other shops and small variants

The provisions shop retains layered depth, substantial timber shelving, an open timber central unit, and a distinct metal entrance rack. Its cutaway makes the till and its relationship to the aisles readable. The fuller wall stock and varied fixture types are useful improvements over the repetitive early shelves.

The produce front maintains coherent per-crate red, green, orange and pale wood families, mixed timber and transport-crate supports, and a clear right-side lane toward the till. The 1.8 by 2.4 m cutaway shows a compact entrance checkout beside a single-column produce display, with the remaining lane visibly open. Its smaller display is an appropriate consequence of reserving payment and access space. This visual observation is not a measured body-clearance or navigation certification.

## Remaining art differences

- **Density:** production shelves have useful depth, but product spacing and repeated rows remain more regular than the concepts. Produce reads as separate tray-sized units; the concept has larger overlapping leafy masses. Preserve clear routes when improving that difference.
- **Grime:** broad brown tile mottling and a repeated horizontal dirty band dominate the restaurant. The reference's finer grout staining, edge chips and localized deposits feel more tactile. Plain dark interior floors are especially visible in the new layout views and still lack convincing tile scale and traffic wear.
- **Color:** crate-to-crate variation works. The four identical saturated red produce shades and regular pale hanging banana groups create a stronger repeating pattern than the reference's mixed muted fixtures. This is an art limitation, separate from the functional kitchen correction.
- **Lighting:** production relies on broad ceiling illumination. The concept concentrates warmer light on ducks, counters and goods and leaves quieter surroundings. Presently the front case, till and steel worktops have less focal emphasis. More global orange tint would not substitute for localized light.
- **Facade:** the large upper fascia patches and relatively orderly awning remain simpler than the concept's dense, fine wear. This shared exterior difference does not invalidate the new shelf-scale patina or justify restoring large rust flakes to small shelving members.

## Assessment

The compact galley, one-sink/two-wok equipment arrangement, dining separation and boundary scaling succeed visually. Request the already identified rear gas-cylinder obstruction correction before final layout acceptance. The default frontages are a useful procedural interpretation, with remaining material, lighting and organic-produce differences; do not claim full visual equivalence to the generated art. Geometry tests, collision and navigation are separate review scopes. No production source was changed by this reviewer.
