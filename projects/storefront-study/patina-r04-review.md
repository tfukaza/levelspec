# Patina R04 — independent visual review

## Verdict

**The whole-room grime request is not yet met.** R04 slightly strengthens brown variation in the restaurant's lower tile field and room edges, but the change is too restrained at the delivered storefront camera. Shelf recesses remain successful: dark backs and undersides give depth while labels stay readable. Preserve that shelf treatment and the daylight exposure.

## Required improvements

1. **High — visible wall deposits, especially restaurant.** The left cream tile wall above the green dado and rear tiles around the cooking equipment still read primarily as a clean regular grid. The counter skirt has much stronger, identifiable dirt. R04's faint broad brown tint does not close that mismatch. Add irregular brown deposit boundaries, a few vertical runoff trails, and localized greasy/sooty patches near the cooking zone. Preserve some clean cream tiles between deposits. Do not solve this by darkening the entire tile field evenly.

2. **High — produce checkout wall remains bare.** Above and behind the checkout and hanging scale, the left wall is still a large smooth gray plane. Add water-stain edges around the lower/middle wall, dirt near the shelf supports and an uneven ceiling/corner deposit. The fruit banks now provide sufficient volume for this pass; the next visible improvement comes from surface character, not more objects obstructing the central passage.

3. **Medium — exposed floors lack accumulated surface detail.** Liquor's wide visible floor still reads as broad lit/shadowed areas with little material history. Use localized stains near fixture bases and perimeter edges with a lighter worn traffic route. Do not confuse straight cast-shadow boundaries with AO artifacts or blur the real shadows to disguise them. The screenshots alone do not isolate which floor transition comes from the vertex field.

4. **Secondary art gap — lighting rhythm and stock regularity.** Liquor's rear upper shelf retains a concentrated warm hotspot while the left bank stays subdued, and the repeated shelf/stock rhythm remains more regular than the concept. This does not justify globally reducing shelf occlusion: exposed labels and front edges are currently readable. A later fixture/stock pass should vary local groups and redistribute practical light through visible sources.

## What should remain

- Strong shelf cavities and visible labels.
- Daytime readability, blue-gray exterior shadows, and warm local practical lights.
- Produce side-bank density and the open central route.
- Subdued kitchen metal rather than the earlier directional noise pattern.

R04 is a modest surface improvement over R03, not a completed match to the reference or the user's stronger whole-room dirt direction. The lack of broad urban context remains a separate concept-fidelity limitation. No geometry, navigation, or collision conclusions are drawn from this visual review.
