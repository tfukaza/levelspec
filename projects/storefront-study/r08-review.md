# R08 critical reference review

Reviewed all three frozen reference/render pairs generated with `node projects/storefront-study/compare.mjs r08`, then inspected the full provisions capture. The pairs preserve complete images and aspect ratios without retouching. This is a visual review, not geometric collision or navigation certification.

## Shelf and frontage pass

Accept the direction of the shelf pass. The actual provisions shop visibly contains deeper stocked decks, open steel wall racks, a lower steel center unit, a narrow entrance tower and retained timber storage at the rear. The former full-height timber end panels no longer dominate the room. Depth remains legible through successive stocked layers. This addresses the user's shallow, repetitive, all-wood complaint with different geometry and proportions rather than color substitutions alone.

R08 also makes the requested frontage correction visible: the left bottle-crate group is taller, and the two small outdoor stands carry bottles and tins instead of orange trays. The shop now reads more consistently as provisions/liquor. The entrance remains visibly open. In this capture there is a substantial visible separation between the taller front crate tops and hanging packets; I see no packet/crate contact. That observation applies to this seed and view only.

The source still has broader and more densely overlapping threshold masses, particularly the right goods tower. The procedural entrance tower is narrow and evenly stocked. The center unit's top cartons partly raise its silhouette again, and much of the retained timber is at the rear. These are refinements rather than reasons to undo the new shelf families.

## Remaining differences from the concepts

1. **Foreground material wear remains the largest shared gap.** Cream piers, charcoal plinths and the near awning edge are substantially smoother and cleaner than the reference. Large peeling islands on the top beam do not replace the reference's smaller chips, handled edges, dirty joints and brown pavement-level deposits. The clean isolated platform and cut-off slab also differ from the surrounding architecture in the concept.
2. **The duck display remains the weakest trade match.** Dirty ivory tiles, the half-glazed green door, hanging birds and a deep kitchen establish the type, but the birds are too small, regularly spaced and stylized for the reference's broad, heavy amber forms. Their weak highlights and subdued task lamps leave the case visually empty. More prominent plate stacks, chopping mass and localized warm lighting would contribute more than additional rear shelves.
3. **Produce is dense enough, but still too regular.** The front banks now read as a stocked grocer. Repeated rectangular trays, small separated produce groups, modest banana hands and the repeated red lamps retain a schematic quality. The right opening is useful and should stay clear. Changes here should concentrate on organic silhouettes and grouping, coordinated with the produce asset work.

Lighting keeps stock readable and the rear receding. Darkening everything would not solve the remaining material or density differences. Package accents and crate colors provide useful variety, although repeated packet strings remain more orderly and bright than the target.

## Assessment

The deeper mixed-shelf requirement and the R08 provisions frontage correction are visibly successful. The overall result is a stronger procedural interpretation, not a close reconstruction of the concepts. Fine foreground weathering and the roast-duck case remain substantive art gaps; tests and renderer verification cannot substitute for those visual differences.
