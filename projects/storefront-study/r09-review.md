# R09 critical reference review

Reviewed all three frozen reference/render pairs produced by `node projects/storefront-study/compare.mjs r09`, then inspected the full-resolution provisions and roast captures. The paired images preserve the complete sources and aspect ratios without retouching. This is an art review; geometric support and clearance claims belong to the separate tests.

## Acceptance of the current shelf pass

The latest user complaint is visibly addressed. Provisions now contains open steel wall shelving, a lower central unit, a narrow entrance rack and retained timber back storage. Stock occupies deeper decks, and successive fixture layers make the room continue into the building. The solid repeated timber cabinet ends that dominated the earlier versions are gone. The taller crate group and packaged frontage goods also read more convincingly as a provisions shop. No additional wholesale shelf redesign is warranted by this comparison.

Some regularity remains: most metal frames use similar gray-green finishes and shelf spacing, and cartons raise the central unit's overall silhouette above its low frame. These are refinements rather than a failure of the shelf-family change. Preserve the useful entrance and room depth instead of filling every visible floor patch.

## R09 changes

- The larger birds materially improve the roast case's fullness. Their amber bodies now occupy more of its vertical display region, with distinct hanging heights. No obvious glazing, countertop or neighboring-bird intersection is visible in the capture. This visual observation does not replace the mesh clearance tests.
- Entrance soot is a sensible localized treatment, but its visible effect is modest at the comparison scale. It does not yet give the broad cream piers, black plinth or pavement contacts the broken, heavily handled character of the reference. Do not describe this change as having solved the grime discrepancy.

## Remaining reference gaps

1. **Foreground wear remains the largest shared material gap.** The source has chipped and rubbed lower walls, dense small deposits at frame joints, damaged awning edges and irregular pavement grime. The procedural version still concentrates its strongest age signal in the oversized peeling patches on the top beam. Its clean cream columns and nearly continuous dark plinth contrast with those patches. Further work should target these small contact regions, not add more large peel islands.
2. **The roast shop's identity still depends on a visibly stylized repeated bird mesh.** Larger size helps the composition but also makes the smooth, repeated body and leg silhouette easier to see. The source's irregular folded wings, necks and greasy surface detail cannot be recovered by scale alone. The source also has prominent warm bulbs and pools of light; the procedural case remains evenly illuminated, with small gray shades and restrained focal highlights. Counter trays and plate groups are still much less substantial than the reference.
3. **The produce frontage remains organized into a very regular tray grid.** Density and crate color variation are present, and the right entrance remains useful. The source has broader overlapping leafy and root masses, larger banana hands and fewer visually dominant repeated lamps. The four bright red shades and small evenly spaced hanging groups still feel schematic. This is primarily a foreground asset/composition issue, not a reason to undo the deeper mixed rear shelving.
4. **The study framing differs from the reference environment.** The large blank fascia, isolated pavement slab and missing neighboring building context affect perceived realism. This is separate from production shelf quality; a tighter crop alone would not repair the material and organic-model differences.

## Assessment

Accept the deeper, varied shelving pass and the R09 improvement in duck-display fullness. The shop models now communicate substantially more depth, stock and fixture variety. The overall scenes remain procedural interpretations rather than close photoreal reconstructions; material microdetail, organic mesh fidelity and localized warm lighting are the principal remaining limits. No production files were edited for this review.
