# R06 critical reference review

Compared all three frozen R06 captures against their concept images using `node projects/storefront-study/compare.mjs r06`. The paired images retain the complete images, preserve aspect ratio, and use equal panel sizes without retouching. This is a visual fidelity review, not a navigation or collision assessment.

## Assessment

Further changes are required for a close reference match. The rooms now communicate real interior depth, but most of the visible age remains broad stains on otherwise clean, straight surfaces. The reference's crowding depends on a dense threshold, mixed fixture silhouettes, and overlapping irregular stock, not only a filled rear wall.

The user's shelf criticism is supported by this capture. The provisions shop repeats tall, dark timber cabinets, including an especially prominent solid center end panel. Rear/side shelving is shallow relative to the reference's deep merchandise layers. R07's planned deeper timber, steel and wire families directly address this; no duplicate shelf rewrite is requested here.

## Highest-impact gaps after the queued shelf work

1. **Weather the foreground at more than one scale.** The side piers and charcoal plinth are nearly clean compared with the reference. The top beam has conspicuous large peeling islands, while the handled edges, threshold, gate joints and lower wall have little small-scale wear. Add localized brown damp, dusty abrasion and chips to these close surfaces. The awning needs a less even hem and smaller worn marks within its faded bands; broad dark stripes alone do not convey aged canvas.
2. **Strengthen trade-specific frontage.** The provisions reference has taller stacked bottle crates at left and a dense open rack at right. Its current little orange trays outside both sides read as generic produce props and dilute the liquor/provisions identity. The produce front has enough overall mass but its crisp continuous crate wall and shallow, separated trays still look arranged as a display grid. Favor broken heights and stock spilling visually across tray boundaries while preserving the entrance.
3. **Give the duck case a stronger warm focal hierarchy.** The reference has substantial glossy amber birds, warm visible lamps and a busy counter. R06's birds are comparatively small, thin and regularly spaced; the body silhouette still resembles the reused stylized chicken form. The case reads as a large bare rectangular cage around them. Larger irregular food masses, warmer localized highlights and more legible crockery/chopping groups would contribute more than further background stock. This is an asset and lighting limitation, not a reason to undo the deeper room.

## Trade observations

- **Provisions:** The brighter package variety is readable, but dark identical timber sides dominate the interior structure. The center bank creates depth at the cost of a large unbroken vertical slab. The two little foreground fruit tables remain unlike the reference. The rear is fairly dark already; simply dimming the whole shop would conceal stock rather than solve its regularity.
- **Produce:** The leafy/root additions are useful, but repeated shallow trays and uniform rows are still obvious. Hanging banana hands are much smaller and more schematic than the reference. Four bright red lamps introduce a repeated saturated motif absent from the target, while the pale left wall remains a large empty region. The right opening remains visible and should be retained.
- **Roast duck:** The half-glazed green service door supplies a useful identity cue. The tiles now read as dirty ivory, but their strong repeated grid and broad gray-brown wash are more graphic than the reference's fine irregular deposits. The actual preparation area is deep, yet the long bare case and small meat silhouettes make the storefront feel sparse. The reference's warm lamps are a major missing visual emphasis.

## Rendering and comparison limits

The closer camera improves inspection, but R06 still presents a cleanly cut-off top slab, isolated pavement platform and neutral background. The concept crop includes more architectural context and weathered edges. Those differences affect perceived realism independently of the shop fixtures. Keep the paired full-frame evidence; do not treat a tighter crop as a geometry improvement or claim the current result is a close reconstruction.
