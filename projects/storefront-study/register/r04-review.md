# Register R04 visual review

Decision: accept this hybrid register for the procedural shops at their ordinary viewing scale. It is materially closer to the supplied concept than the former box-built register and the unrepaired Tripo mesh. It is not an exact close-up reproduction of the concept; the authored controls still need wear integration for a hero presentation.

## Evidence inspected

- Concept: `projects/kowloon-restaurant-props/concepts/kitchen-props/register.png` (AI concept, not a rendered asset).
- Frozen R04: `generated/storefront-study/register-r04/register-operator.png`, `register-customer.png`, and `roast.png`.
- Earlier R03 operator/customer captures as iteration context.
- Paired comparison: `generated/storefront-study/register-r04/register-reference-comparison.png`.
- Targeted source inspection: `projects/storefront-study/register-details.mjs`, receipt thickness and rear display placement.

## Findings

The rounded cream housing, drawer separation, printer hump and sloped key deck preserve the concept's recognizable silhouette. The new central keycaps have consistent silhouettes and spacing instead of the visibly folded Tripo controls. The dark red and green function keys remain restrained against the cream body. The readable lower operator display helps the register read as equipment rather than a blank block.

The R04 customer display now stays inside the broad rear shell. It no longer bridges the raised printer-cover seam as R03 did. Its subdued panel and digits are visible from the customer side without a tall monitor. The receipt appears connected to its slot and curls naturally; the short strip does not dominate the silhouette. The source now gives its underside a 0.2 mm separation and closes the side/end edges, removing the earlier coincident front/back surfaces. No source geometry concern was found in these local changes.

In the full shop capture, the register sits credibly on the checkout end, stays a supporting detail, and retains a readable cream silhouette. The keyboard is necessarily below text-reading scale there. Contact shadow and surrounding scene shadows anchor the object. The sharp side triangle was investigated by the separate R03 reviewer: it disappears with other scene casters disabled and is not baked into the albedo or caused by the imported normal map. It should not be painted out of the asset.

## Concrete remaining improvement

The authored keycaps and legends are visibly cleaner and more uniform than the housing and the concept. This is strongest in the central ivory bank and the repeating E/F/C rows on the right bank in `register-operator.png`. Uniform intact bright lettering and single-color cap surfaces make the manual replacement read as a newer component. For a future close-up polish pass, apply restrained rubbed-away legend edges and slight finger-wear variation to the most-used numeric/enter keys, with subtle dirt in cap seams. Give the function bank meaningful grouped abbreviations instead of repeating one letter across a row. Keep the corrected geometry and regular spacing; do not reintroduce lumpy keys to simulate wear.

This is a residual close-up art gap, not a blocking defect at the inspected shop scale. No broad store-density, layout, lighting, or gameplay acceptance is implied by this register review. Test results remain separate functional evidence.
