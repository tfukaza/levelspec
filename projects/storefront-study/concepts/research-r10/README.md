# Hong Kong shop studies

These references inform an invented Kowloon / Ghost in the Shell atmosphere, rather than reconstructing a specific real business. Photographs establish functional relationships; generated concept art proposes colors, wear, lighting and density. Neither source establishes exact dimensions or certifies circulation.

## Photograph observations

- [Bo Kee, Michelin](https://guide.michelin.com/hk/zh_HK/article/features/story-of-champagne-court): deep stock-lined interior, substantial dark timber, ceiling fan and tightly packed goods. Local study: `generated/storefront-study/research-photos/bo-kee-michelin.jpg`.
- [Greg Girard, Kowloon counter photograph](https://www.greggirard.com/content/gallery/girard_kowloon008.jpg): pale working counter, phone and ledger, timber storage cubicles and tiled walls. Local study: `generated/storefront-study/research-photos/kowloon-counter-greg-girard.jpg`.
- [Joy Hing, DanielFoodDiary](https://danielfooddiary.com/2018/03/27/joyhing/): display/preparation beside a narrow dining zone, tables and stools, cashier space and overhead fans. Local study: `generated/storefront-study/research-photos/joy-hing-daniel-food-diary.jpg`.
- [Street vegetable stall, The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/hong-kong-news/article/54926/Colonial-road-signs-lost-to-old-neighborhoods): densely grouped produce, varied overhead fittings and improvised labels. Local study: `generated/storefront-study/research-photos/vegetable-stall-the-standard.jpg`.

Photo rights remain with their respective creators/publishers. These are reference evidence, not textures distributed with production models.

## Generated concepts

`prompts.json` retains the three original prompts and reference inputs. `provisions.png` and `produce.png` are active studies. `roast.png` was rejected because the kitchen was too large, sinks repeated and woks missing. `roast-v2.png`, generated with `roast-v2-prompt.txt`, is the corrected duck-shop concept. All are AI-generated inspiration, not screenshots of the procedural model; small plan insets can disagree with their perspective views.

Actual production captures, model hashes, cameras, and paired comparison images live under `generated/storefront-study/r10/` and subsequent revisions. Independent critiques are retained in the parent study directory. Widths, aisle reservations, fixture footprints and furniture counts are authored choices checked against the generated geometry.
