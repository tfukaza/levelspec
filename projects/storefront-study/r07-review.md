# R07 critical reference review

Reviewed all three frozen R07 reference/render pairs made with `node projects/storefront-study/compare.mjs r07`, then inspected the full-resolution provisions capture. The comparison preserves complete images, aspect ratios and equal panel sizes. This is visual assessment, not proof of fixture support or navigation.

## Shelf requirement

R07 visibly addresses the user's complaint. The provisions shop now has open, aged metal wall shelving, a lower central steel unit, a narrow tall entrance rack, and a timber bank at the rear. The central solid wooden end panel is gone. Stock reads in deeper layers, with bottles and larger cartons occupying substantial decks rather than lining a shallow cabinet. This is a meaningful change in actual rendered geometry, not only a material recolor.

The families could still be more legible as different pieces of furniture: the narrow wire tower and large steel banks share similar thin gray outlines from this camera, and the retained timber is mostly relegated to the back. Repeated level spacing and long rectangular banks remain obvious. The lower center unit's carton stack also raises its overall silhouette again, though it remains more open than R06. These are refinement opportunities; another wholesale shelf replacement is unnecessary.

## Remaining high-impact gaps

1. **The threshold is still undersized relative to the reference.** The right entrance rack is an improvement, but the reference has a broad, densely layered goods tower and chest-high left crate masses. R07's small bottle-crate group and two little orange tables leave a much more open frontage. The orange tables also weaken the provisions identity. Use bottle/carton/tin displays and taller flanking stacks while retaining the aisle; do not fill the useful depth with a wall of stock.
2. **Foreground age is still much weaker than the large top-beam stain.** Smooth cream piers, a nearly unbroken charcoal plinth and regular awning hem contrast with the reference's rubbed edges, chips, dirty joints and brown pavement-level deposits. More large peeling islands on the top slab will not solve this. Concentrate smaller, localized wear on the visible contact surfaces.
3. **The duck case still lacks the reference's warm food focus.** R07 has dirty ivory tiles and the green half-glazed door, but its small, evenly spaced, stylized birds sit inside a disproportionately bare glass rectangle. The reference's large amber forms, warm lamp pools and substantial plate/chopping groups carry the composition. The deeper pantry is useful, but additional remote shelving will contribute less than those front-case changes.

## Other trade observations

- **Produce:** Mixed rear shelving is visible through the opening, although the large foreground produce banks naturally conceal much of it. The main density is now adequate. A very regular crate grid, small separated produce groups, small banana hands and four bright red lamps still make the scene schematic. These are front-display and asset issues rather than a failure of the new back-stock shelving. Preserve the right-hand opening.
- **Lighting and color:** The provisions stock is legible and the rear recedes. Do not darken the entire room to imitate grime. Its gray-green metal is a better neutral framework than the former dark timber slabs. Package saturation and repeating hanging packets still feel more regular than the target. The duck scene needs localized warm highlights rather than a uniform exposure increase.
- **Presentation:** The isolated pavement platform, clean cut-off top slab and missing surrounding architecture remain differences from the reference crop. They should not be mistaken for shelf defects, and cropping cannot substitute for geometry or materials.

## Assessment

Accept the direction of the shelf-depth and shelf-variety change: the improvement is clear in the actual shop. Further focused art work is needed for a close overall reference match, especially frontage proportions, fine foreground wear and the roast-duck display. R07 is a stronger procedural interpretation, not yet a close reconstruction.
